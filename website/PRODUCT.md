# shut website

## Users

People on Bluesky deciding whether to subscribe, and people who want to run or check the labeller.

## Product purpose

Answer three questions quickly: what gets hidden, where it gets it wrong, and how to switch it on.
The try-it box lets a visitor test the real detector without sending text anywhere.

## Brand

**Tone:** plain, calm and direct. British English. No outrage, no mockery of anyone.

**Identity:** a shop door sign. shut is a door you can choose to close.

**Anti-references:**

- Not a safety-tech sales page with metrics and testimonials
- Not a wall of examples of the language it hides
- Not the docsite terminal, the devlog's amber timeline, the Hasharium cabinet or the riso zine

## Strategic principles

1. **Never overstate.** If no labeller is running, say so. State the misses and false positives.
2. **Never display slurs or real posts.** Describe rule categories and link to the source. The one
   example match uses an ideological term, not a slur.
3. **Private by default.** No analytics, cookies or third-party requests.
